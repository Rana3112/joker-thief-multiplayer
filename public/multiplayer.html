How to Play:

  • Setup: Create a room or join with a room code
  • Goal: Don't be caught with the Joker at the end!
  • Pairs: Two cards with the same rank and color (♥♦ or ♠♣)
  • Turn: Auto-stock pairs, then select and give one card to the next player
  • Endgame: The player left holding only the Joker is the "thief" and loses
